Look up at the observatory tower — it stands out easily against the Sorbonne's façade.

The tower is covered in bullet holes, far more visible than on the main façade you were just looking at.

The tower gave height, and the balcony at the top made it a perfect place to set up machine guns — exactly what both the German troops and the resistance fighters wanted to control. The marks you can see are what's left of that. These aren't the first bullet holes you've seen on this tour, which tells you how ferocious the fighting was along the route you've been walking.

Turn around, and keep going straight. We're almost at Notre-Dame.
