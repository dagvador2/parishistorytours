You're now at the base of the Sorbonne observatory tower. Look up at the stonework.

The tower is covered in bullet holes — far more visible than on the main façade. Take a moment to count them.

This was one of the most important nests of fighting on the Left Bank during the insurrection. The tower offered height and a clear line of fire — exactly what both German troops and resistance fighters wanted control of. The marks you can see are what remains of those days.
