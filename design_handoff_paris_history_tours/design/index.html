Your Guide · Independent Historian

Clement Daguet-Schott

A passionate history enthusiast and independent tour guide based in Paris since 2024. After months of dedicated research across twenty-plus historical books, memoirs, and primary sources, I built three immersive walking tours that bring Paris to life — from the Fall of France and the French Resistance to two thousand years of Parisian history.

Whether you're a curious traveler, a history buff, or an educator, these tours are designed to make history tangible. Rigorous research, engaging storytelling, iconic landmarks — walked at a comfortable pace, told with time for questions.
